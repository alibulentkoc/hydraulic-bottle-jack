Questions 2 to 13 of the handout, worked from the values above. Pressure here is W / A_r, the load alone, as the handout has it; the lifting pressure in Live values also carries the ram's own weight.

For an ideal machine, hand power, hydraulic power and lifting power are one number: the ideal Power_in row equals Power_hyd and Power_out exactly. The arc method of question 11 departs from it by a theta / s_p, which is theta / sin(theta) when s_p, a and theta agree with each other and something else when they do not.

Governing relationships

A = pi * D^2 / 4
P = W / A_r
V_p = A_p * s_p
A_p * s_p = A_r * h
h = s_p * (A_p / A_r)
MA = b / a

Force is multiplied by the area ratio and distance is divided by it. Watch the ram rise per stroke shrink as you enlarge the ram: that is the same trade, seen as motion.
