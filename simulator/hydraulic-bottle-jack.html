Governing relationships

A = pi * D^2 / 4
P = F / A_ram
V = A_pump * s_pump
A_pump * s_pump = A_ram * s_ram
s_ram = s_pump * (A_pump / A_ram)
MA = L_handle / L_pivot-to-link

Force is multiplied by the area ratio and distance is divided by it. Watch the ram rise per stroke shrink as you enlarge the ram: that is the same trade, seen as motion.
