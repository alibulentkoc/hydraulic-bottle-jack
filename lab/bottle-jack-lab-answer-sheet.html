Model assumptions. All results are ideal and quasi-static: no friction in the linkage or seals, no internal leakage past the check valves, no fluid compressibility, and no losses in the passages. A real jack requires more hand force and delivers less output power than these numbers predict. Units are US customary throughout (in, lb, psi, hp), with 1 hp = 550 ft-lb/s = 6600 in-lb/s.
