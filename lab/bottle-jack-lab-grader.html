Hydraulic bottle jack laboratory - measurement audit and answer check

Enter the master dimensions you measured for the jack this group was assigned, then the values they reported. Each dimension is checked against your master within the tolerance you set, and every derived answer is recomputed twice: once from your master dimensions, and once from theirs.

Why the second column matters. A group whose measurement is off will get a different answer, and that answer can still be arithmetically correct. Separating the two lets you credit correct method on wrong data, which is the usual case, and lets you spot the opposite pattern: answers that do not follow from the numbers reported alongside them.
