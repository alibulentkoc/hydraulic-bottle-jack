Hydraulic Bottle Jack

Interactive cross section and laboratory materials

A bottle jack is the smallest complete fluid power system in common use: reservoir, pump, check valves, directional control, actuator, and passages, performing a full hydraulic cycle in a device you can hold in one hand.

Interactive cross section Pump the handle and watch oil move through the circuit. Every dimension is editable, and the ram rises only by the volume the pump actually delivers.
